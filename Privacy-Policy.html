Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. This policy applies to umabellows.com.

What we collect

When you submit the Request a Quote form on this site, the details you enter — name, company, email address, phone number, product of interest, and message — are sent to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-delivery service that forwards them to our inbox at contact@umabellows.com. FormSubmit receives this information only to deliver it to us; how it handles data is described in the FormSubmit privacy policy.

If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp, or email directly, we receive whatever information you choose to share through that channel.

How we use it

Information you submit is used only to respond to your enquiry — to prepare a quotation, answer a technical question, or follow up on a project. We do not sell your contact details or share them with third parties other than the form-delivery service described above, and we do not use them for marketing you haven't asked for.

Cookies & analytics

This site does not currently set tracking cookies or run third-party analytics. If that changes, this policy will be updated to disclose what's collected and why.

Data retention

Enquiry correspondence is retained as part of our normal business email records for as long as needed to service the relationship and meet our accounting and legal obligations.
