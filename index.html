About Uma Bellows

Full-cycle, under one roof

At Uma Bellows, design, fabrication, and testing never leave the building. Our engineers work each configuration to spec; our fabrication floor welds to standard using MIG, TIG, and SMAW procedures; and every unit is verified on our own testing floor before it ships.

Over seventeen years, Uma Bellows has built out full in-house capability — engineers who design to spec, fabricators who weld to standard, and a testing floor that runs cyclic life, rupture, squirm, radiographic, ultrasonic, and leak testing before anything ships. That discipline is why our client list spans national infrastructure groups and government utilities that don't have room for a part that fails.
