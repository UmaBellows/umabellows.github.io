Expansion Bellows with Liners

A lined expansion bellows has a smooth internal sleeve fitted just inside the convolutions, so the flowing media passes over the liner rather than directly over the flexible bellows material.

Representative example — real Uma Bellows expansion joint:

How it works

Convolutions are inherently more exposed to erosion and turbulence than a smooth pipe wall would be; a liner protects them from direct high-velocity or abrasive flow, extending the bellows' service life without changing its flexibility.

  • Internal liner shields convolutions from direct flow contact
  • Reduces erosion in high-velocity or particulate-laden service
  • Does not add stiffness — the bellows still flexes normally
  • Liner design accounts for its own thermal expansion within the joint
