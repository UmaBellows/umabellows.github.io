High-Cycle Bellows

A high-cycle bellows is engineered and verified specifically for applications where the joint moves — cycles open and closed — far more often over its service life than a typical thermal-expansion duty would require.

How it works

Fatigue life under repeated cycling is a different design and testing question from static pressure rating alone, which is exactly what Uma Bellows' in-house cyclic life testing exists to verify before a unit ships — real capability, not a marketing claim.

  • Engineered for a specified number of movement cycles, not just pressure rating
  • Verified with in-house cyclic life testing before shipment
  • Ply count and material selection tuned for fatigue life
  • Relevant wherever movement is frequent rather than occasional
