Expansion Joint Configurations

Metal Expansion Joint

"Metal expansion joint" is the general industry term for an expansion joint built around a convoluted metal bellows element — the category every specific configuration on this site (axial, lateral, gimbal, and the rest) belongs to, as distinct from rubber or fabric expansion joints used in lower-pressure, lower-temperature service.

How it works

Every configuration described on this page is a metal expansion joint at its core — the differences between them are in how the bellows is mounted, restrained, and oriented, not in the underlying convoluted-metal working principle.

  • Umbrella term covering every configuration on this site
  • Distinguishes from rubber and fabric expansion joints
  • Suited to higher pressure and temperature duty than non-metallic types
