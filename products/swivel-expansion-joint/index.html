Expansion Joint Configurations

Swivel Expansion Joint

A swivel expansion joint allows the piping on either side to rotate about the pipe's own longitudinal axis — a rotating flange or bearing arrangement takes the twisting motion so the bellows convolutions themselves are never subjected to torsion.

Representative example — real Uma Bellows expansion joint:

How it works

Because the bellows is isolated from the rotational load by the bearing, it continues to do only what convoluted bellows do well — flex axially or laterally — while the swivel hardware handles alignment rotation separately.

  • Rotation happens at a bearing, not in the bellows convolutions
  • Used for rotational alignment adjustment in piping runs
  • Keeps the bellows element free of torsional stress
  • Distinct from a torsional bellows, which flexes under twist directly
